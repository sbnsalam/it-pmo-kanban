    Quick start

    1. Add a task with the + button at the top right. A panel slides in from the right; title, assignee and due date are required.
    2. Move a task by dragging its card to another column, or pick a column from the card's Move ▸ menu.
    3. Delete a task with the × on its card, then confirm with Yes. Choose No to keep it.
    4. Filter the board by project, assignee or priority. Column counts show filtered tasks; the header chips always count everything.
    5. See progress on Burn-down, the view the page opens on: open tasks against an ideal line, weekly completions and a projected finish. Board, next to it above the filters, shows the Kanban columns.
    6. Switch theme with the sun / moon button. The board follows your system setting until you choose.

    Columns

    • Backlog: logged, not yet scheduled.
    • In Progress: actively being worked.
    • Blocked: waiting on a dependency, vendor or approval.
    • Done: completed or closed.

    Card colours

    The left edge of each card and its pill show priority: red for Critical, amber for High, blue for Medium and grey for Low. An Overdue badge appears when the due date has passed and the task isn't Done.

    Where is my data saved?

    Nowhere permanent. Tasks live in memory only, so refreshing the page resets the board to the demo tasks, and nothing is stored in your browser. The one exception: when you add a task, its details are sent to FormSubmit so the notification email can be delivered.

    Who gets an email when I add a task?

    Each new task sends one notification through FormSubmit to the address set in FORMSUBMIT_ENDPOINT at the top of the script. It's the only thing that leaves the page.

    Why didn't the notification email arrive?

    FormSubmit needs a one-time activation. The first task sends a confirmation email to the configured address, and nothing is delivered until someone clicks its link. If sending fails, the card stays on the board and a warning appears.

    Can I move cards without a mouse?

    Yes. Every card has a Move ▸ menu that works with the keyboard and screen readers.

    Why can't I pick a due date in the past?

    New tasks must be due today or later. Some demo tasks are already overdue so you can see how overdue work is flagged.

    Why don't the column counts match the header?

    Column counts respect your filters. The header chips always count every task on the board, including how many are overdue.

    Does the theme choice stick?

    Only until you refresh. The board stores nothing, so it goes back to following your system light/dark setting.

    Is this a real bank system?

    No. It's a demo and training tool for a fictitious bank's IT PMO. The projects, people and tasks are made up.

    A through-line is the thread that connects a story from start to finish. The logo shows three Kanban lanes with an amber line carrying work forward.
